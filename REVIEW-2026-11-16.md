Scope per review brief: defensive code-quality review, cryptographic correctness,
documentation, safe testing. **No offensive/stealth capability was added and the wire

## Reviewer's claim — verified status

**AES-256-CTR without authentication (confirmed as described; design limitation, not a
code bug).** `shared/crypto.py` uses RSA-2048-OAEP(SHA-256) to wrap a per-session
AES-256 key and AES-256-CTR (random IV per message, IV prepended) for traffic —
correctly implemented confidentiality, but CTR is malleable: bit-flips in ciphertext
produce predictable plaintext flips and `decrypt()` cannot detect modification.
Consequences and future options (AES-256-GCM AEAD, forward secrecy, passphrase-protected
key file) are now documented, not silently absent.
